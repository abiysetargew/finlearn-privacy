Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

FinLearn ("we", "our", "the app") is an educational app for learning financial markets. This privacy policy explains what data we collect — which is almost nothing, intentionally.

Data we do not collect

  • No personal information (name, email, phone)
  • No account, no login, no signup
  • No usage analytics sent to us
  • No crash reports sent to us
  • No trade data sent to us
  • No journal entries sent to us
  • No location, no device ID, no advertising ID
  • No contacts, no photos, no microphone, no camera access

Data stored on your device only

The following lives only on your device and is never transmitted to us:

  • Lesson progress (mastered / in-progress)
  • Streak count and streak freezes
  • XP score and level progression
  • Journal entries (text)
  • Simulator trades (numbers)
  • Trading Room picks
  • Onboarding answers (motivation, experience, daily goal)
  • Notification preferences (toggle states + times)

To delete everything: uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings → Apps → FinLearn → Storage → Clear data. This wipes all on-device data.

Network requests

The app includes a Market News and Economic Calendar feature that can fetch financial headlines and scheduled NBE / ESX / ECMA events.

When you open those screens, the app makes read-only HTTPS requests to retrieve that public information:

  • The request contains no personal information — no account, no identifier, no advertising ID, no device fingerprint.
  • The request does not include anything stored in your journal, your simulator trades, or your lesson progress.
  • The response is cached only in memory for the duration of the screen. It is not written to your device and not associated with you.

Everything else in FinLearn — the entire 131-lesson curriculum, the charts, the pattern library, the simulator, the flashcards and the Trading Room — works fully offline and makes no network requests at all.

Children's privacy

FinLearn is rated for everyone but is not intended for children under 13 (no age gate implemented in this version). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, regardless of age.

Third-party services

  • No third-party analytics (no Firebase, no Sentry, no Google Analytics)
  • No advertising SDKs (no AdMob, no Meta, no Unity Ads)
  • No payment processors (no Stripe, no Chapa)
  • No data brokers

Permissions

The app requests:

  • android.permission.INTERNET — used only to fetch the public market headlines and economic-calendar events described above.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE — for haptic feedback on taps. This does not transmit any data.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS — for optional local reminders (daily streak, evening practice). Notifications are scheduled on your device and contain no data.

The app does not request:

  • LOCATION, CAMERA, MICROPHONE, CONTACTS, STORAGE, READ_CONTACTS
  • Any background location or accessibility service

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the current version. If changes are material, we will display a notice in the app.
